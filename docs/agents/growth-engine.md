## What It Does

The Growth Engine is not a generic marketing agent. It is a developer-tools growth specialist who understands that developers have hype antibodies. It knows that "revolutionary" gets you downvoted on Hacker News, that copy-pasting content across platforms is malpractice, and that vanity metrics (impressions, followers) mean nothing while pipeline metrics (installs, activations, retained users) mean everything.

It operates across every growth channel with platform-native playbooks: Hacker News (Show HN anatomy, response strategy, timing), Reddit (karma-building before self-promotion, comment voice rules, subreddit-specific tactics), Twitter/X (build-in-public threads, demo videos, engagement ratios), Product Hunt (launch day choreography, hunter selection, post-mortem), LinkedIn, Dev.to/Hashnode, and GitHub itself as a growth channel (README optimization, issue templates, awesome-list inclusion).

Beyond channel tactics, it provides strategic growth frameworks: content calendar creation with SEO-informed topic selection, open source viral loop analysis (discovery, README conversion, time-to-value, aha moment, share trigger), community building infrastructure, email drip campaign design, competitive positioning, and analytics/attribution setup. Every output ends with a specific decision and a measurement plan -- intelligence, not information.

## When to Use It

- **Launch planning**: When you are preparing to launch on Hacker News, Product Hunt, Reddit, or any developer community and need a platform-specific playbook.
- **Open source growth strategy**: When your GitHub repo has great code but nobody finds it, and you need a systematic plan to grow discoverability and adoption.
- **Content marketing**: When you need a quarter's content calendar with topic research, keyword targets, distribution channels, and publishing cadence.
- **Competitive positioning**: When competitors are shipping similar features and you need to find your differentiated angle backed by evidence.
- **Post-launch analysis**: After any launch (successful or not), when you need a structured post-mortem to learn what worked and what did not.

Do not use this agent for visual design (Design Vanguard) or pricing strategy (Revenue Architect). The Growth Engine drives adoption; other agents handle the product and revenue surfaces.

## How It Works

1. **Context Gathering**: Understands the ask -- which channel, what stage of growth, what has been tried before.
2. **Current State Audit**: Reads the repo, README, existing content, and analytics to establish a baseline.
3. **Landscape Research**: Uses web search to check competitors, trending topics, and keyword volumes for informed planning.
4. **Strategy Building**: Applies channel-specific playbooks and growth frameworks to create a specific, actionable plan.
5. **Deliverable Writing**: Produces content calendars, launch playbooks, post drafts, and audit reports as structured files in `.claude/growth/`.
6. **Measurement Setup**: Defines metrics, creates tracking templates, and establishes baselines with UTM parameter standards.

## Power Use Cases

**Platform-Native Content Generation**: The Growth Engine does not write one piece and blast it everywhere. It adapts content to each platform's culture and norms. A Reddit comment uses casual voice, one paragraph, no formatting. A LinkedIn post has a hook in the first line and short paragraphs. A HN comment leads with technical depth. This platform-native approach prevents the "AI marketing speak" that gets content flagged and downvoted.

**Funnel Analysis and Optimization**: Map the full growth funnel -- Awareness (found the repo), Interest (read README), Activation (installed, ran first command), Retention (used again within 7 days), Advocacy (starred, shared, contributed) -- and identify where conversions drop. If Week 1 retention is below 30%, the onboarding is broken, and no amount of top-of-funnel content will fix it.

**AI Content Guardrails**: The Growth Engine maintains a strict blacklist of AI-detectable words and phrases (delve, tapestry, robust, holistic, seamless). All generated content must pass the human smell test -- varied sentence lengths, strong opinions over balanced analysis, specific numbers over generic claims, platform-native voice over polished prose.

## Tips & Gotchas

- **Do**: Build genuine community presence (karma, comments, helpful answers) on a platform BEFORE any self-promotion.
- **Do**: Run a post-mortem after every launch, including failed ones. The failures teach more than the wins.
- **Don't**: Cross-post identical content to multiple platforms. Each platform has its own culture and format.
- **Don't**: Measure stars and followers. Measure installs, activation rate, and weekly retained users.
